Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Who we are

DALIGO is a delivery booking service in Butuan City, Philippines. Contact us at [YOUR EMAIL HERE] for any privacy question.

What we collect

Customers:

  • Name, mobile number, and email address.
  • A photo of your valid ID and the type of ID, so we can verify your account.
  • Pickup and drop-off addresses, map pins, item details, and notes for the rider.
  • Your phone location, only when you tap "Use my location" on the map.

Riders:

  • Name, mobile number, email address, and motorcycle plate number.
  • Photos of your driver's license, OR, CR, and the front and back of your motorcycle.
  • Your live phone location, only while you have an active job.
  • Photos you take as proof of pickup and delivery.

Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We cannot see them.

Why we use it

  • To check who our customers and riders are before they use the service.
  • To arrange, track, and complete deliveries.
  • To keep proof of pickup and delivery, handle complaints, and keep everyone safe.

Who can see it

  • ID photos and rider documents: only you and the DALIGO administrator.
  • Riders see the customer's name, phone number, addresses, and notes for the jobs assigned to them.
  • Customers see the assigned rider's name, phone number, and live location during their delivery.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (login and database) stores your data.
  • OpenStreetMap shows the map. When you pin a location, its coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap to find the address.

How long we keep it

We keep your ID and documents while your account is active, and delete them when you delete your account or ask us to. Booking records are kept for up to 12 months for proof and support, then removed.

Delete your account

  • Customers: open the app, go to Profile, and tap Delete my account. Your account and ID photo are removed.
  • Riders or anyone who cannot use the app: email [YOUR EMAIL HERE] from your registered email address and ask for deletion. We will act within 30 days.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), you may ask to see, correct, or delete your personal data, object to its use, and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

Children

DALIGO is for people 18 and older.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
