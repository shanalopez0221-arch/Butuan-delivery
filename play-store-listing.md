## Full description
DALIGO - Padala. Pickup. Dalihin.

Need something delivered in Butuan City? Book a DALIGO rider in a few taps.

- Pin your pickup and drop-off on the map, and the address fills in for you
- See your estimated fare before you book (PHP 50 per km)
- Send parcels, food, documents, and more
- Pay in cash or GCash
- Watch your rider on the live map and call them
- See photo proof at pickup and at delivery
- Every rider is checked: license, OR/CR, and motorcycle plate

Currently serving Butuan City.
